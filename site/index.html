Our story

From the lanes of Gikomba to your wardrobe

We're not a big fashion house. We're a small hustle that wakes up early, walks the market and picks the pieces worth wearing.

Why Gikomba?

Gikomba is East Africa's biggest second-hand clothes market. Bales of mitumba from the UK, Europe, the US, Canada and Asia are opened here every morning, and the mix is unlike anything in a mall.

The catch: good pieces go fast, and a lot of what's in a bale isn't worth your money. We go early, dig, and only keep what passes our check: no stains, no holes, zips and soles that work.

We're upfront about the deal. Our prices are a little above the stall price. That covers the early mornings, the washing, the photos and getting it to your door. You still pay far less than new.

Questions people ask

Can you hold an item for me?+

Every piece is one-off, so it goes to whoever pays first. Once you've confirmed on WhatsApp we can hold it for a few hours while you arrange payment.

What if it doesn't fit?+

Tell us your usual size before paying and we'll measure the item for you. Mitumba sizes differ by country (UK, US, EU), so we always double-check.

Are the clothes washed?+

Yes. Outfits are washed and ironed. Shoes are cleaned inside and out before dispatch.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?+

Yes, anywhere in Kenya through courier and parcel shuttle services. Most towns get it in 2 to 4 working days.

Can I pay on delivery?+

Payment is via M-Pesa before dispatch. For CBD pickup, you can see the item first and pay on the spot.

Quick tip

Everything is one-off, so the faster you message, the better your chances. If a piece is sold, ask for something similar and we'll look in the next bales.
